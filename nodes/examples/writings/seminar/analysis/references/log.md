## Summary of connections drawn

**analysis1.md** (8 threads): Benzon's "prototypical story" attractor (paper1) is the same phenomenon as his own "20 things" list experiments in our subject paper — repeated minimal prompts settling into the same conceptual territory. The reference student's finding that single-session runs stay coherent while separate-session runs sometimes degenerate into token salad (on Llama-8B) parallels our own sidenote's butterfly/dragonfly "50 more" list-collapse experiment; their fix (more specific prompts) suggests an unexamined variable in our sidenote's drift. Their DeepSeek-vs-Llama comparison empirically confirms a training-corpus-dependence claim our subject paper only speculates about. Both the reference student and our own sidenote independently push back on Benzon's small-n, hand-picked case studies with the same fix: larger N, controlled/matched sampling. A tangential connection to an unrelated autonomous-driving paper was noted as genuinely thin.

**analysis2.md** (9 threads): paper2 already draws its own black-box/white-box distinction before the presentation (via Haralick & Ramesh) relabels it as "performance characterization." The "some story segments change, others don't" coherence phenomenon in paper2 echoes the inheritance/assignment-relation logic our subject paper's Appendix cites. The presentation's flagged session-history gap is something Benzon partially self-diagnoses in paper2's experiment 6 and, more directly, in our subject paper's own repeated-list and repeated-20-Questions reproducibility checks. The "20 versions" default-archetype finding (pulled in from a third Benzon paper) parallels our subject paper's narrow-attractor list results and our own sidenote's list-degradation experiment. Presentation2's proposed rigor fixes (n stories, controlled sampling, statistics) are structurally the same issue→fix→result move our sidenote made for the 20-Questions abstract/concrete bias.

**analysis3.md** (12 threads): confirmed reference paper 3 is Benzon's own 1985 CIM report, self-cited in our subject paper's Appendix — so this analysis is framed around the direct authorial link. Strongest connections: the 1985 paper's assignment tree (object→plant→animal→human→social person) is literally the Great Chain of Being our subject paper spends pages probing in ChatGPT (cross-referenced by Benzon's own footnote); the salt/NaCl and Morning-Star/Evening-Star examples are reused near-verbatim across both papers, 38 years apart; the subject paper's ChatGPT "Apple" 20-Questions transcript empirically enacts the 1985 paper's substantive-vs-functional paradigm split; the subject paper's hedging about ChatGPT's "depth" of understanding anticipates Naco's "sidestepped it" verdict; and our own sidenote's controlled rerun of the abstract/concrete 20-Questions asymmetry (finding no bias once vocabulary sampling is balanced) directly undercuts the subject paper's headline finding and independently corroborates Naco's skepticism. One point (water/H2O) doesn't actually appear in the 1985 paper's text — traced instead to the subject paper's own extension of the salt/NaCl example.
