3. Written Assignment 
Each student will write a short analytical text (1200-1800 words) after their 
presentation. This text should: 
● Discuss the core argument and contribution of the paper they 
presented. 
● Analyze how their paper relates to the other papers presented in 
the same seminar session. 
● Reflect on broader themes, unresolved questions, or tensions across 
the presented works. 
This approach is intended to foster critical thinking and synthesis of ideas, 
moving beyond simple summary or peer review. Please note that we are 
looking for discussion of the general subject of the symposium not a 
summary of the paper you presented! 
There is no strict form that we expect your essay to adhere to. However, if 
useful, see the following blog posts for inspiration Intro to LLMs, 
Unsupervised Models of the Brain, Generalized Visual Language Models.
