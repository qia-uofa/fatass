# Tone Guide

Distilled from a side-by-side comparison of an AI-drafted paragraph and
the user's rewrite of the same content (a summary of Benzon's "ChatGPT's
Ontological Landscape"). Concrete rules over self-report — each point
below is backed by an example from the diff.

## Formality

- Academic register throughout. Swap casual framing for precise
  academic phrasing: *"an old philosophy example"* → *"a classic
  paradox."*
- Default toward the fuller, uncontracted form in formal exposition —
  the rewrite drops most of the original's contractions. Not an
  absolute ban, just the default lean.

## Emotional register

- Neutral and matter-of-fact. No injected enthusiasm — *"and I think
  it's the most interesting thing in the whole paper"* is cut entirely
  rather than softened.
- Evaluative or striking claims are attributed to the source as its
  claim/thesis, never framed as the writer's own live reaction.

## Earnest vs. ironic

- Earnest and straightforward throughout. No irony, no humor, no
  rhetorical flourish, no "clever" turns of phrase.

## Address

- Impersonal third person. No direct address to a "you," and even the
  writer's own opinion is reframed as reported analysis: *"The refusal
  marks the thesis of Benzon"* rather than *"I think..."*.

## Avoided

- No sarcasm, no condescension, no casual editorializing folded into a
  descriptive sentence.
- A little jargon is fine when it's doing real explanatory work
  (*"syntactically/semantically"*, *"epigenetic landscape"*) — the line
  is unnecessary jargon, not jargon per se.

## Reference example

- Original (casual, personal): *"That refusal is the paper's starting
  point, and I think it's the most interesting thing in the whole
  paper: a system trained only to predict the next word behaves as
  though it respects a category structure that nobody built into it on
  purpose..."*
- Revised (flat, academic): *"The refusal marks the thesis of Benzon: a
  system trained only to predict the next token behaves as though it
  respects a category structure that was not referenced by it."*

This pair is the clearest single illustration of the tone shift this
guide describes: drop the personal aside, keep the claim, flatten the
delivery.
