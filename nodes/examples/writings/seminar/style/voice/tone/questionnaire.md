# Tone Questionnaire

**Tone** is the attitude a piece of writing takes toward its subject and
its reader — the emotional register carried by word choice, sentence
rhythm, and address (formal vs. casual, warm vs. detached, earnest vs.
ironic). Fill this out to describe the tone you want your writing
analyzed against or written in.
