# Section manifest — *An Ontological Perspective on LLM Beliefs*

One file per top-level chapter of `plan/structure/_.md`. Indices give each
section's position in that outline; front matter and back matter are counted
as sections in their own right because the plan lists them as top-level units.

Target lengths are apportioned by each chapter's scope in the plan against the
field convention recorded in `assets/format/_format.md` (roughly 30–50 pages of
main text; no page or word count is mandated by PO 2019 §35 or the Prüfungsamt
Merkblatt). The reproduction, method and results chapters carry the empirical
weight and are budgeted accordingly; the framing chapters are kept tight.

## Notes on the apportionment

- Chapter 5 gets the largest single budget because the plan gives it eight
  subsections covering ten experiments plus a self-audit, and because it is one
  of the two chapters carrying original empirical work.
- Chapters 6 and 7 are budgeted together at 5,300 words: the plan splits the
  extension into a method chapter with seven subsections and a results chapter
  with eight, and the extension is where the thesis's own contribution sits.
- Chapter 3 is deliberately shorter than Chapter 2 because most of the
  technical vocabulary it would otherwise have to introduce is defined in the
  background chapter instead.
- Chapter 9 is budgeted at only 1,000 words because the substantive limitation
  arguments are made where the results are reported, and repeated here in
  compressed form rather than restated at length.
