1. **Introduction** — motivate the question (do LLMs "know" more than they say?), state contributions (replication, extension, philosophical/linguistic connection), preview findings.

4. **Methodology** — datasets, models, your replication procedure, then your extension (new models/tasks/metrics — whatever you decide).
5. **Results** — replication results first (sanity-check against original paper's numbers/trends), then extension results.
6. **Discussion** — interpret findings; this is where the philosophical/linguistic connection lives — e.g., what does the internal/verbalized gap mean in light of theories of assertion, sincerity, hedging, etc.
7. **Limitations**
8. **Conclusion & Future Work**
