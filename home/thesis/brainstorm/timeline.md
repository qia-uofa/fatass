| 9 (final week, submit by week's end — earliest submission is week 4) | Revision + submission | Full read-through, advisor feedback incorporated, formatting/binding per Merkblatt (3 printed copies + PDF, signed Erklärung), submit with buffer before the hard deadline |

## Buffer / risk notes
- Earliest submission is week 4 — don't submit early even if writing is done; use the extra time for polish or a second extension round if replication is slow.
- If replication (weeks 1–2) overruns, the extension (RQ3) is the part to cut back first — it's explicitly scoped as secondary in the profile.
- Illness/justified-delay extensions (up to 50%, i.e. +32 days) must be filed *before* the deadline — don't wait until you're already late.
- Concluding presentation (Oberseminar) should happen within 4 weeks of submission — pencil that in once you have a submission date.
